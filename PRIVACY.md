# RustSheet Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 3, 2026_

RustSheet is a spreadsheet app that runs entirely on your computer.

- **No data collection.** RustSheet does not collect, store or transmit personal information, usage data, analytics or crash reports.
- **No network access.** The app makes no network connections.
- **Your files stay yours.** Workbooks are read from and saved to locations you choose on your device. RustSheet does not upload them anywhere.
- **No third parties.** No data is shared with or sold to anyone, because none is collected.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Divhanthelion/rustsheet/issues.
